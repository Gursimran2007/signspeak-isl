How it works

  1. The model tracks 21 points per hand from your webcam.
  2. It compares your hand shape to signs you taught it.
  3. Hold a sign steady to commit it, then it’s spoken aloud.
⚠️ No signs trained yet. Switch to Train signs and teach it a few (try HELLO, YES, NO) — takes ~30 seconds.

Teach a sign

Type a name, make the sign, hold Record while you slightly move/rotate your hands. Special names SPACE and DELETE act as keys.
